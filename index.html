Short-form video studio

From raw gameplay clip to a finished vertical video.

auto is a desktop studio for creators. It helps you find promising moments in your recordings, cut them into vertical short videos, add readable captions, and publish to your own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Find the moment

Scene and audio signals plus a transcript help pick the part of a long recording that is actually worth posting.

Cut for vertical

Automatic 9:16 reframing, 1080×1920 output, burned-in captions and normalized audio, ready for short-form feeds.

Check before publish

Duration, resolution and file integrity are validated automatically. Clips that fail the checks never reach your account.

Publish on your terms

Preview the result, edit the title and hashtags, choose privacy and interactions, then confirm the upload yourself.

How it works

  1. Add your footage. Point auto at a folder of recordings or import a clip you own.
  2. Let the editor propose a cut. The app reads the transcript and scene/audio signals and suggests a window inside the duration you configured.
  3. Review the preview. Watch the vertical result, adjust the title and hashtags, and keep or redo the cut.
  4. Connect TikTok once. Sign in with TikTok through OAuth. auto only asks for basic account information and permission to publish videos.
  5. Confirm the post. The publish screen shows the account, the available privacy options and the interaction settings. Nothing is uploaded until you press Publish.

TikTok integration

auto uses two official TikTok for Developers products: Login Kit for authentication and the Content Posting API (Direct Post) for publishing. No passwords are ever handled by auto.

Login Kit

The connection uses the OAuth 2.0 authorization-code flow with a state parameter and an HTTPS redirect URI. auto requests user.info.basic only, to display the nickname and avatar of the account that will receive the video. Tokens are stored on your machine and refreshed before they expire. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions.

Content Posting API

Before showing the publish form, auto queries the creator information endpoint and displays the authorized account. The privacy dropdown only contains the options TikTok returns for that account, and it starts empty — you must choose. Comment, Duet and Stitch are unchecked by default and are disabled when TikTok reports them unavailable. The publish screen also includes the commercial-content disclosure and the music usage confirmation, and requires an explicit confirmation before any data is sent.

Uploads use the file-upload flow, are split into chunks, and report progress through TikTok's publish-status endpoint so you can see when processing finishes. auto does not add watermarks, logos or promotional text to your video.

auto publishes only to the account you authorize. It does not create accounts and does not post on behalf of third parties.

Privacy and security

auto stores OAuth tokens and video files locally on the user's machine, protected by the operating system user account. There is no analytics, advertising identifier or tracking pixel, and no personal data from other TikTok users is collected. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Frequently asked questions

Does auto post without my confirmation?

No. The publish screen requires an explicit confirmation after you review the preview, the account, the privacy level and the interaction settings.

Which TikTok permissions does it request?

user.info.basic to show the authorized account, and video.publish to upload a video after you confirm.

Can I choose who sees each video?

Yes. The privacy options come from TikTok for the authorized account. While an app has not completed TikTok's audit, TikTok itself restricts API posts to private viewing; the app shows the options TikTok returns and never overrides them.

Does auto add a watermark?

No. Captions are burned into the frame for readability, but there is no app logo, handle or promotional watermark on the video.

How do I disconnect my TikTok account?

Revoke auto in TikTok's app permissions, then delete the local token file. Future uploads stop immediately.
